Strategy, shoot, edit, ad buying and reporting sit with me. Nothing gets lost between departments, and there is no agency markup.

Honest reporting

Questions

Are you an agency?

No. I am one person, Yedukrishna Suresh. I plan the campaign, shoot it, edit it, buy the ads and report on them myself.

What if a single person is not enough for a big shoot?

For larger productions I bring in the specific extra help the shoot needs, booked for that job. You still deal with me from start to finish.

Do you work in Dubai and India?

Yes. I work with brands in the UAE and India, and I am based in Dubai from mid-November 2026.

Do I pay the ad platforms directly?

Yes. Your ad money goes from your account straight to Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn or OpenAI. My fee is separate and agreed up front.

How do you report results?

In plain language: what ran, what it cost, what it brought in, what I stopped and what I would do next. You also keep full access to your own ad accounts.

How fast can we start?

Usually as soon as we agree the plan and the shoot date. Message me on WhatsApp and I will tell you honestly what timing is realistic.

Tell me what you sell and roughly what you want to spend. I will reply with an honest plan.
