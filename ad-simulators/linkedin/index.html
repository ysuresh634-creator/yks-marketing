The formats

How LinkedIn ads work

Sponsored content (feed)

A post in the feed marked "Promoted", with your company name, the text, an image or video, and a headline bar with a button.

Message ad

A message in the LinkedIn inbox, marked "Sponsored", with a button. It works best when it reads like a person wrote it.

What it costs

UAE benchmarks put LinkedIn at about AED 25 to 116 per 1,000 views and AED 5 to 18 per click. In India it is about ₹450 to 1,400 per 1,000 views and ₹120 to 650 per click. LinkedIn’s minimum is USD 10 a day per campaign.

Questions

How much do LinkedIn ads cost in Dubai?

Public UAE benchmarks put LinkedIn at about AED 25 to 116 per 1,000 views and AED 5 to 18 per click, with a minimum of USD 10 a day per campaign.

How much do LinkedIn ads cost in India?

Published Indian benchmarks range from about ₹450 to 1,400 per 1,000 views and ₹120 to 650 per click.

Why is LinkedIn so much more expensive?

You are paying to reach people by job title, seniority and company. One right click on LinkedIn can be worth far more than many cheap views elsewhere.

Can I see my competitors’ LinkedIn ads?

Yes. The LinkedIn Ad Library is public. Search a company name to see the ads it has run.

Is this an official LinkedIn tool?

No. It is an illustration I built to explain the formats. It is not affiliated with LinkedIn.
