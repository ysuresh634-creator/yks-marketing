The formats

How TikTok ads work

In-feed ad

A full-screen vertical video in the For You feed with your name, a "Sponsored" tag and a button. It has to work like a TikTok, not like a TV ad.

TopView

The first video shown when someone opens TikTok. Sold as a reservation through TikTok’s sales team rather than a small self-serve budget.

India: the Government of India blocked TikTok on 29 June 2020 and the ban is still in place; in September 2025 the IT minister said there was no proposal to lift it. So this simulator only prices the UAE.

What it costs

Dubai benchmarks put TikTok at about AED 8 to 45 per 1,000 views and AED 0.40 to 9 per click. TikTok also sets minimums: AED 185 a day per campaign and AED 75 a day per ad group.

Questions

How much do TikTok ads cost in Dubai?

Public UAE benchmarks put TikTok at roughly AED 8 to 45 per 1,000 views and AED 0.40 to 9 per click, with a minimum of AED 185 a day per campaign.

Can I run TikTok ads in India?

No. TikTok has been blocked in India since June 2020, and the government said in September 2025 that it had no plan to lift the ban. For India, Instagram Reels and Snapchat are the closest options.

Where can I see real TikTok ads?

TikTok Creative Center’s Top Ads page shows real, high-performing ads. You can filter by country, including the UAE, and by industry.

What is a TopView ad?

The first full-screen video that appears when someone opens TikTok. It is reserved in advance through TikTok, so it suits big launches rather than small budgets.

Is this an official TikTok tool?

No. It is an illustration I built to explain the formats. It is not affiliated with TikTok.
