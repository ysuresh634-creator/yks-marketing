The formats

How Snapchat ads work

Snap Ad

A full-screen vertical ad between friends’ Stories, marked "Ad", with your name at the top and an attachment that opens your site, app or a form when people swipe up or tap.

Story Ad

A branded tile in the Discover feed. Tapping it opens a short series of Snaps, which suits a product range or a step-by-step story.

What it costs

Dubai benchmarks put Snapchat at about AED 5 to 12 per 1,000 views and AED 0.30 to 1.20 per swipe-up. In India it is about ₹100 to 320 per 1,000 views and ₹2 to 12 per swipe. Snapchat’s own minimum is USD 5 a day.

Questions

How much do Snapchat ads cost in Dubai?

Public UAE benchmarks put Snapchat at roughly AED 5 to 12 per 1,000 views and AED 0.30 to 1.20 per swipe-up. Snapchat’s own minimum is USD 5 a day.

Are Snapchat ads worth it in India?

They can be for younger, city audiences. Indian benchmarks are about ₹100 to 320 per 1,000 views and ₹2 to 12 per click. Test against Instagram Reels before you commit.

What is the swipe-up on a Snap Ad?

It is the attachment. When people swipe up or tap the card, Snapchat opens your website, app store page, a lead form or a longer video.

Where can I see real Snapchat ads?

Snap runs a public Ads Gallery and a political ads library. Both are linked on this page.

Is this an official Snapchat tool?
