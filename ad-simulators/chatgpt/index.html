The formats

How ChatGPT ads work

Where it shows

Below a ChatGPT response, separate from the answer. OpenAI says each ad has your advertiser name, favicon, a title, a short description, an image and your landing page.

Who sees it

OpenAI says ads are not shown on Plus, Pro or Business plans, or to accounts identified as under 18. So you reach the free and Go tiers.

How you pay

Views (per 1,000), Clicks, or Conversions (still billed per click or per view). OpenAI suggests starting click bids at USD 3 to 5.

Who can buy

OpenAI’s availability page lists both the United Arab Emirates and India as available for self-serve. The business that is billed must be based in a listed country, and some new accounts can only target their home country at first.

What it costs

This is new, so there are few public numbers. Early reports (mostly US tests) put ChatGPT ads at about USD 25 to 65 per 1,000 views and USD 1.72 to 9.89 per click. The minimum daily budget in Ads Manager is AED 65 or ₹725.

Questions

Can I run ChatGPT ads in Dubai or India?

Yes. OpenAI’s Ads Manager availability page lists the United Arab Emirates and India as available for self-serve. The billed business must be based in a listed country.

What does a ChatGPT ad look like?

A card below the answer marked "Sponsored", with your name, favicon, a title, a short description, an image and a link. It is kept separate from the answer itself.

How much do ChatGPT ads cost?

Early public reports put them at roughly USD 25 to 65 per 1,000 views and USD 1.72 to 9.89 per click, mostly from US tests. The Ads Manager minimum is AED 65 or ₹725 a day.

Who sees ChatGPT ads?

Users on the free and Go plans. OpenAI says it does not show ads on Plus, Pro or Business plans, or to accounts identified as under 18.

Is this an official OpenAI tool?

No. It is an illustration based on OpenAI’s published description of the ad unit. It is not affiliated with OpenAI.
