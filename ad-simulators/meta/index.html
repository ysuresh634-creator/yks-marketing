The formats

How Meta ads work

Instagram feed (4:5)

Shows between posts with your name, a "Sponsored" label and a call-to-action bar under the image. 4:5 takes the most screen.

Instagram Story (9:16)

Full screen between friends’ Stories, with a button at the bottom. People tap through fast, so the first second matters most.

Instagram Reel (9:16)

Full-screen video in the Reels feed with the like, comment and share column on the right. Usually the cheapest views on Meta.

Facebook feed (1:1 or 4:5)

Text above, image, then a link bar with your headline and a button. Still strong for older audiences and local services.

What it costs

In the UAE, public benchmarks put Meta at about AED 10 to 35 per 1,000 views (Facebook cheaper, Instagram higher) and AED 0.60 to 2.50 per click. In India it is roughly ₹50 to 250 per 1,000 views and ₹4 to 50 per click; Reels tend to be cheapest and the feed dearest.

Questions

How much do Instagram ads cost in Dubai?

Public Dubai benchmarks put Instagram at roughly AED 18 to 35 per 1,000 views and Facebook at AED 10 to 25, with clicks at about AED 0.60 to 2.50. Your real cost depends on the audience, season and the ad itself.

How much do Instagram ads cost in India?

Published Indian benchmarks range from about ₹50 to 250 per 1,000 views and ₹4 to 50 per click. Reels are often cheapest (around ₹30 to 80 per 1,000 views) and the feed costs more.

Can I see what ads my competitors run?

Yes. Meta’s Ad Library is public. Search a brand name and you will see every active ad it runs on Facebook and Instagram, with the start date.

Is this an official Meta tool?

No. It is a preview I built to explain the formats. It is not affiliated with Meta, and real layouts vary a little by device and app version.

Why are the numbers a range?

Because nobody can promise an exact number before an ad runs. The range comes from published low and high costs, so you can see the realistic spread.
